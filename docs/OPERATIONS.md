# Operational Workflow

## Customer

1. Submit a complaint with Complaint Type, description, address/location, and optional complaint photo.
2. Track the complaint timeline and notifications.
3. Respond if Commercial Services requests additional information.
4. Reopen an eligible resolved complaint when the issue persists.
5. Submit resolution feedback.

## Commercial Services Department

Commercial Services owns intake/review activities:

1. Review the complaint and classifier result.
2. Correct/override operational priority with a required reason when necessary.
3. Request missing customer information when necessary.
4. Link/merge duplicates before field work begins.
5. Add an optional handoff note.
6. NSCCCD sends field-related complaints to WDLCD under ECMD.
7. Handle Commercial billing/account tools, customer advisories, and Commercial reports.

## ECMD

ECMD owns field coordination:

1. Receive forwarded complaints in Complaint Dispatch.
2. Review map/location, priority, workload, and Maintenance Personnel availability.
3. Assign/reassign Maintenance Personnel or crews.
4. Monitor field progress, related complaints/incidents, manpower, materials, and operational notes.
5. Review Maintenance completion notes.
6. Verify completion or return the complaint for additional work.
7. Verification changes the complaint to **Resolved**.

## Maintenance Personnel

Maintenance Personnel do not perform an acceptance step. Assigned work appears directly in their task workspace.

They can:

- view assigned complaint/task details and location;
- update field-work status;
- record progress notes, manpower, and inventory usage;
- request assistance/reassignment;
- submit completion notes.

Completion by Maintenance Personnel does not by itself resolve the customer complaint. ECMD performs final verification.

## System Administration

System Supervisors manage system governance only:

- Staff Accounts and department assignment
- audit/security events
- System Administration announcements
- approvals/archive recovery
- backup-verification records
- System Health

System Supervisors do not automatically receive Commercial or ECMD operational workspace access.
